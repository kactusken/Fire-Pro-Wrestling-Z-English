# Changelog

## v0.1 (2026-10-31)
First public beta.
- The whole game in English: menus, seven story scenarios, wrestlers, moves and menu graphics.
- Two name sets, real names and the game's parody names, translated.
- *All Unlocked* patches: every unlock from the start, the hidden Liger scenario finished with real
  matches, seven extra rings, six extra moves and the dojo venue.
- A four-page player's guide, with walkthroughs and a route map for every story.
